# Facilities snapping cross-check (HydroWASTE v1.0 vs snapped ICIS outfalls)

Bottom line: an independent georeferencing of the same plants lands 8,731 of 10,546 AOE HydroWASTE outfalls within 1 km of an ICIS outfall (median separation 98 m, p90 582 m).

- HydroWASTE AOE plants: 10,546; ICIS outfalls: 410,138
- separation over matches: median 98 m, p90 582 m, max 999 m
- disagreements beyond 500 m (eyeball these): 1,172
- HydroWASTE plants with NO ICIS outfall within 1 km: 1,815 (vintage drift, non-NPDES facilities, or our clip)

Evidence quality: both coordinate sets are measured products; HydroWASTE is a static 2021 academic dataset (see reference/water-treatment-facilities/), ICIS refreshes weekly -- treat disagreement as a QUESTION about a specific plant, never as either source's verdict.
